# Patch notes

## 1.0.9.1

- The author now shows as **DTM Mods, PropaineX** in the mod list.

## 1.0.9.0
- New icon with "PARKING BRAKE" written on it.

## 1.0.8.0
- When the brake is off, only the BRAKE word stays half lit; the symbol goes dark.

## 1.0.7.0
- BRAKE is easier to read when the brake is off.

## 1.0.6.0
- The dash light also comes on when anything else holds the brakes (AI helpers, AutoDrive, Courseplay).

## 1.0.3.0 to 1.0.5.0
- Dash light resized to match the gearbox icon; BRAKE text made smaller.

## 1.0.2.0
- Dash light that looks like a real truck parking brake warning light.

## 1.0.1.0
- Parking brake light on the speedometer.

## 1.0.0.0
- First release: press \ to set or release the parking brake on any drivable vehicle. A warning blinks if you press the throttle with the brake on. Works in multiplayer and is saved with the game.
